Café Komorebi — Character Sheet

The main cast drawn in 2D with the café's palette, the same plum outline and the same flat cel shading as the 3D diorama. Switch the time of day to check how they sit in front of the café, and use the buttons on each card to play their animations.

Every costume colour comes from the café: the uniforms are espresso brown or black with brass details like the café's gold sign trim, Moka's apron and the takeaway cups match the brown uniform, Biscuit's bandana is the red post box, Grandma's cardigan is the shutter blue, and Kettle wears the fascia green band. Outlines everywhere use the diorama's ink colour #3b2838.
